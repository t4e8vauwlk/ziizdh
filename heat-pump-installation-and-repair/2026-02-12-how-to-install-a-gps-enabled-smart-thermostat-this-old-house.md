Pro Tip: On a basic heating system, you’ll typically find just a red and a white wire. The base plate terminals are clearly labeled—R for red, W for white. But if you have a furnace with air conditioning, you could have five, six, or even seven wires.

How GPS Geofencing Works: A GPS-enabled smart thermostat communicates with your smartphone’s GPS. When the last person leaves the house, it automatically detects that everyone is gone and sets the system back to a lower temperature. As you get close to home, it senses your return and starts bringing the house back to your comfort setting — fully automatic, with no need to manually override from an app.

Pro Tip: When removing the old thermostat, check if the new unit’s base plate is a different size or shape. If the outline of the old thermostat is visible in the paint, the manufacturer may include a trim plate to cover the patch. But if you have matching paint on hand, it looks much nicer to skip the trim plate and simply touch up the wall before mounting the new unit. Resources
