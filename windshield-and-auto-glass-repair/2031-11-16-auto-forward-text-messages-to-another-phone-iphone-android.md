Would you like to automatically forward text messages to another phone from your iPhone or Android? It might be a good idea if you own both a personal and a work phone, for example. Unfortunately, automatically forwarding SMS messages from an iOS to a non-iOS device is not possible. However, Apple makes it easy to forward both SMS and iMessages from your iPhone to other iOS devices. Android users can also automatically forward messages with ease. This wikiHow guide will teach you how to automatically forward all incoming text messages from your Android or iPhone to another device. Things You Should Know

Forward iMessages or SMS/MMS texts from one iPhone to another by signing into the same Apple ID across all devices.

Navigate to Settings > Messages > Text Message Forwarding and select all devices you want to receive forwarded texts.

Install the SMS Forwarder app on Android to set up the automatic forwarding of messages to a phone number or email address. Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2: Forward from iPhone to iPhone Download Article 1

Sign in with the same Apple ID across all devices. To automatically forward all iMessage and SMS texts to other iOS devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac, etc.), you must be signed in with the same Apple ID on each device.[1]XResearch source

To confirm which Apple ID you are signed in with, navigate to Settings > Messages > Send & Receive and look for the listed Apple ID.

All devices must meet Apple's Continuity requirements to receive SMS/MMS messages.

iPhones, iPads, or iPods receiving texts must be running iOS 8 or later.[2]XResearch source 2

Enable Text Message Forwarding on your iPhone. Navigate to Settings > Messages > Text Message Forwarding on the iPhone from which you want to forward texts.[3]XResearch source

If you do not see "Text Message Forwarding" as an option, turn off iMessage and turn it back on.

If this does not resolve the issue, you may not be signed into your Apple ID on any other devices. Double-check that you are signed in with the same Apple ID across all the iPhones you want to receive texts. Advertisement 3

Choose the device(s) you want to receive forwarded texts. The "Text Message Forwarding" landing page will display a list of all devices connected to the same Apple ID. Toggle the slider to the right (it should turn green) for any devices you want to receive forwarded texts.[4]XResearch source 4

Enter the verification code from each device on your iPhone. Unless your other phones are using multi-factor authentication, they will display a verification code that needs to be entered on your iPhone. Verify each device to start forwarding your texts![5]XResearch source

You can send and receive SMS/MMS messages from any of the forwarded devices, as long as your original iPhone is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi or a mobile network.

iPhones cannot automatically forward text messages to non-iOS devices, but you can do it manually.

iPhone users can try Forward SMS to automatically forward texts to emails, Teams, Slack, or Discord. Advertisement Method 2 Method 2 of 2: Forward from Android Download Article 1

Download the SMS Forwarder app from the Google Play Store. There are several SMS forwarding apps available in the Play Store, but we recommend this one here.

This wikiHow guide can help you manually forward texts on your Android.[6]XResearch source 2

Open the app and press +. It is the circular orange button located at the bottom of your screen. 3

Enter the phone number to which you want to forward text messages. You can also enter an email address.

You can enter several numbers/emails, using commas to separate each entry. 4
