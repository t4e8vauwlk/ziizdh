When you’re learning how to keep your carpet smelling and looking new using carpet freshener powder or sprays, you’re likely to have a couple of questions. See the most common ones below. ​

Q: What is the best way to remove a pet odor from a carpet?

The best way to remove pet odor from carpet is to use a natural carpet cleaner made to neutralize pet odors. For persistent smells, a carpet cleaning machine can help fully eliminate them.

Q: Why does my carpet smell like dog pee after shampooing?

Your carpet may smell a bit like dog pee if the carpet shampoo you used doesn’t contain ingredients for neutralizing pet urine odors. Also, if the machine you used doesn’t have strong suction, it’s possible it didn’t fully remove the dirty water and urine residue.

Q: Do different types of carpets require different carpet deodorizers?

Yes, different carpets do often require different carpet deodorizers. While the deodorizing agents themselves are usually safe, certain products may include cleaning ingredients that could discolor, shrink, or stretch certain fibers. Since each carpet freshener​ is different, be sure to read the label before use, and use a plug-in air freshener near delicate fabrics as an alternative if necessary.

Q. How often should I use a carpet deodorizer?

To get rid of odors in your house, apply a carpet deodorizer anywhere from once a week to once a month. If you have pets or kids or are combating odors caused by smoke, food, or mildew, using a non-toxic carpet deodorizer regularly can help.

Q. Do carpet deodorizers leave behind any residue?

Some carpet deodorizers can leave residue behind, especially if they’re powder-based. For residue-free cleaning, choose a spray formula or try a steam cleaner. Q. Do carpet deodorizers really work?

Yes, carpet deodorizers (like a scented powder for carpet​ or this liquid DIY carpet deodorizer recipe) really work on odors, either by masking or neutralizing them. For more comprehensive results, consider a deodorizer that also cleans and sanitizes. Meet the Tester
