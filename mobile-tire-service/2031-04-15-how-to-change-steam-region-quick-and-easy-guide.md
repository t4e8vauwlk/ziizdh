If you're moving to a new country and want to update your Steam region, it's easy! You'll just need to add and use a new payment method from that region, and Steam will take care of the rest. Currently, there is no way to change it through your settings, so make sure you have a valid payment method from the country you want to switch to. Here's what to know about changing your Steam region on your Windows or Mac computer. Updating Your Steam Store Region

To change your region on Steam, click your profile > Account Details to add a new payment method from that region. Purchase any game or content from the Steam store. Your region will be updated automatically to reflect the payment method's region. Steps Section 1 of 2: How to Change Your Region on Steam 1

Launch Steam. The only way to change your region on Steam is to make a purchase using a payment method from the new region. This is different from changing your download region, which is the region used to download your games and updates. If you're using the wrong download region, your downloads may be slower than they should be.[1]XResearch source

You can only update your Steam region once every three months. 2

Click your profile and select Account Details. Your profile name will be in the top-right corner, and you'll see "Account Details" in the drop-down menu. A new page will open. Advertisement 3

Add a new payment method. If you have an existing payment method, you'll click Update the payment method linked to this account. If you don't have a payment method linked, you'll click Add a payment method to this account.

Enter the billing information, such as the card number, expiration date, and address. Then, click Continue to add it to your account.

Make sure the new payment method is from the country you want to change to. 4

Purchase a game or content from the Steam store. Click the Store tab to browse the trending games, or visit your wishlist. You can purchase any game or content (including trading cards). When you see something you want to buy, add it to your cart, and then check out.

Make sure to use the new payment method for the content.

When the purchase is completed, Steam will automatically switch the region to the one matching your payment method. Advertisement Section 2 of 2: Frequently Asked Questions 1

How do I see my current Steam store country? To see what your current Steam store country is, open Steam, click your profile name in the top-right corner, and then select Account Details. You'll see your store country in the bottom-right corner. 2

How do I change my Steam download region? To change your Steam download region, open Steam and click Steam to open the menu. Select Settings and then Downloads. Click the drop-down menu next to Download region and select a new one. 3

How do I change my Steam region if I move? If you move, you can easily switch your Steam region by adding a new payment method from the new region and completing a purchase. 4

What happens if I make an account in another country? If you make a new account in another country, your Steam region will be set to that country by default. However, you can change it back to your home country by adding and using a payment method from your current region. 5

How often can I change my Steam store country? If you travel or move often, you can change your store country once every three months. You can use any local payment method that matches the country currently set on your Steam account. Otherwise, you'll need to wait until this 3-month period is over. 6

Can I use an APO (Army Post Office) address? If you're currently serving abroad, you can use any payment method from the United States to set your Steam Store country to the U.S. Please note that physical hardware purchases can't be shipped to these APO addresses. 7

Why can't I change my Steam region? If you still can't change your Steam region, even after adding a new payment method and completing a purchase, you can contact Steam support.
