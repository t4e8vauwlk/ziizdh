Running out of Bumble matches in your city? It's easy to expand your search distance to include potential matches that are further away. If you're seeing people who are too far away, you can also filter the results to a smaller area. Read on to learn how to adjust your distance filter in Bumble! Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2: Mobile App Download Article 1

Tap the yellow Hive icon. You'll find this at the bottom-center of the Bumble app.[1]XResearch source 2

Tap the filter icon. This icon looks like two sliders, and you'll find it at the top-right corner of the screen.[2]XResearch source This opens the search filters page, where you can modify your distance preferences. Advertisement 3

Drag the "Distance" slider to the desired position. If you want to see potential matches that are farther away, drag the slider to the right. To decrease your preferred distance, drag the slider to the left.

If there is no "Distance" slider, this feature is not available in your region.[3]XResearch source

If you're willing to see matches that are a little further away than the distance you specified, toggle the "Dealbreaker?" switch below the slider to the On (yellow) position. 4

Tap the Apply button. It's at the bottom of the screen. Your new distance preferences will take effect immediately. Advertisement Method 2 Method 2 of 2: Bumble Web Download Article 1

Click your profile picture. It's at the top-left corner of the Bumble homepage.[4]XResearch source 2

Click Settings. You'll see it in the left panel near the middle of the menu.[5]XResearch source 3

Drag the "Distance" slider to the desired position. If you want to include potential matches who are farther away, drag the slider to the right. To see only close-by matches, drag the distance to the left.

You might have to click Filter in the right panel before you'll see the "Distance" slider.

If there is no "Distance" slider, this feature is not available in your region.[6]XResearch source 4

Click the X to close Settings. Your new preferences will take effect immediately. Advertisement Expert Q&A  Search Add New Question Ask a Question 200 characters left
