Before you cut your first piece of drywall, devise a plan whereby you use the largest pieces of wallboard possible. The larger the pieces, the fewer joints there’ll be to feather and finish later.

Another important considera­tion is finding a corner that is square and working from there. You should also make sure that the first piece ap­plied to each wall is precisely plumb.
